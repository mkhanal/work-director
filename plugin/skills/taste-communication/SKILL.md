# Taste: communication

## Verified outcomes are stated deterministically
Once confirmed, say "under state X, Y happens", never "may" or "might". Label unverified claims as such.
**Why:** Hedging on verified behaviour hides which claims are checked.
**Apply:** Two lists if needed: verified, unverified.

## Outcomes first, sized to the reader
Lead with the conclusion. Tables for facts, prose for reasoning. Never narrate your process.
**Why:** The reader decides; the process is yours.
**Apply:** A yes/no scenario table beats three paragraphs when alignment is the goal.
