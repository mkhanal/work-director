# Taste: alternatives

## Every comparison includes do-nothing with its real cost
Compare options against "do nothing" with its actual consequence and how existing behaviour bounds it.
**Why:** Defensive machinery competes against that baseline, not against zero.
**Apply:** Table: option, consequence, bound, agent-minutes. Do-nothing is row one.

## Do not re-open settled decisions
Once a risk is explicitly accepted, state genuinely new information in one sentence and move on.
**Why:** Re-warning and re-litigating waste the decision-maker's attention and tokens.
**Apply:** Keep a decisions list in the brief; treat it as ground truth.
