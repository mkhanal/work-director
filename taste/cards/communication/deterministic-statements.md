id: deterministic-statements
title: Verified outcomes are stated deterministically
category: communication
scope: [global]
kind: practice
status: adopted
always: false
enforce: []
evidence: []
---
Once confirmed, say "under state X, Y happens", never "may" or "might". Label unverified claims as such.
**Why:** Hedging on verified behaviour hides which claims are checked.
**Apply:** Two lists if needed: verified, unverified.
